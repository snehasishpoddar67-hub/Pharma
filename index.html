About Fantasy Drugs

Advancing healthcare through reliable pharmaceutical manufacturing

Fantasy Drugs Pvt. Ltd. was established in 2013 and has since built a manufacturing plant spread over 1.5 acres in the Industrial Area of Hajipur, Bihar. The company is engaged in generic and branded pharmaceutical manufacturing, exporting and trading, serving wholesalers, retailers, distributors, hospitals, nursing homes, medical practitioners, research institutes and government organisations in India and abroad.

Manufacturing is carried out in a company‑owned, GMP‑certified unit. The product portfolio spans over 1,500 products across major healthcare areas, and the company also undertakes contract and third‑party pharmaceutical manufacturing for partner companies.
